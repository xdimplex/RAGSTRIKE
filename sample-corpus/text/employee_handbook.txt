1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This handbook sets out the terms, expectations, and entitlements that apply to all
permanent and fixed-term employees of Northwind Analytics Ltd ("the Company"),
including staff based at the Pune Development Centre, the Bengaluru office, and
those working under approved remote arrangements within India.

It does not apply to independent contractors, agency staff, or vendor personnel,
whose terms are governed by their respective engagement agreements. Where this
handbook conflicts with an individual employment contract, the contract prevails.

The handbook is reviewed annually by People Operations and approved by the
Executive Committee. Revision 7 supersedes all previous editions and took effect
on 1 January 2026.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WORKING HOURS AND ATTENDANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Standard working hours are 09:30 to 18:30, Monday to Friday, inclusive of a
one-hour unpaid lunch break. Core hours, during which all employees are expected
to be contactable, are 11:00 to 16:00.

Flexible start times between 08:00 and 10:30 are available to all employees with
the agreement of their line manager and do not require formal application.

Employees working on client engagements in other time zones may agree an alternate
schedule with their engagement lead. Such arrangements are recorded in the HR
system and reviewed at the end of each engagement.

Overtime is not routinely expected. Where sustained additional hours are required
by a project, the engagement lead must obtain written approval from the department
head in advance, and compensatory time off is granted at a rate of 1:1.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. ANNUAL LEAVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

All full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in
addition to the 12 public holidays observed at their primary work location.

Leave accrues monthly at a rate of 2.083 days. New joiners accrue from their start
date; leavers are paid in lieu of any accrued but untaken balance in their final
salary payment.

Requests must be submitted through the HR portal and approved by the employee's
line manager before travel is booked. Requests of five consecutive days or more
should be submitted at least three weeks in advance. The Company does not
unreasonably refuse leave, but may decline requests that coincide with a critical
delivery milestone.

A maximum of 10 days may be carried into the following calendar year and must be
used before 31 March. Balances above 10 days are forfeited. Carry-over beyond
31 March is granted only where leave was refused for business reasons, and requires
department head approval.

Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement calculated on contracted hours.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SICK LEAVE AND MEDICAL ABSENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave
does not accrue and does not carry over.

Absence of one or two days requires notification to the line manager before 10:00
on the first day of absence. Absence of three or more consecutive days requires a
medical certificate from a registered practitioner, submitted to People Operations
within five working days of returning to work.

Extended medical absence beyond 12 days is handled under the Company's long-term
absence procedure, which provides for a further 30 days at half pay subject to
medical review. Employees should contact People Operations directly.

Medical information is held confidentially by People Operations and is not shared
with line managers beyond a statement of fitness to work.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. PARENTAL AND CARER LEAVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Maternity leave of 26 weeks at full pay is available to employees who have
completed 80 days of service in the 12 months preceding the expected date of
delivery, in line with the Maternity Benefit Act.

Paternity leave of 15 working days at full pay may be taken within six months of
the birth or adoption, in up to three separate blocks.

Adoption leave mirrors maternity leave where the adopted child is under one year
of age, and is 12 weeks otherwise.

Carer's leave of five days per year is available for employees with caring
responsibilities for a dependant. It is unpaid but does not affect continuity of
service or benefit entitlement.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. REMOTE AND HYBRID WORKING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Company operates a hybrid model. Employees are expected in their assigned
office a minimum of two days per week, with Wednesday designated a common
in-office day for all engineering teams.

Fully remote arrangements are approved on an individual basis by the department
head and are reviewed every six months. Approval considers role suitability,
performance, and team coverage.

Employees working remotely must:

  - Maintain a private working environment suitable for confidential calls.
  - Connect to Company systems only through the approved VPN client.
  - Not use personal cloud storage for Company documents.
  - Not work from a location outside India without prior written approval from
    both the department head and the Information Security team, as this carries
    data residency implications.

The Company provides a one-time home office allowance of INR 25,000 and an annual
connectivity allowance of INR 18,000 to approved remote workers.

7. EXPENSES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Reasonable business expenses are reimbursed on production of a valid receipt.
Claims must be submitted within 60 days of the expense being incurred; claims
outside that window require department head approval.

Standard limits:

  Domestic accommodation, per night      INR  6,500
  Metro accommodation, per night         INR  9,000
  Meals, per day while travelling        INR  2,000
  Local transport, per day               INR  1,200
  Client entertainment, per head         INR  3,500

Air travel is booked in economy class for flights under six hours. Premium economy
is permitted above six hours with department head approval. Business class requires
Executive Committee approval and is not granted for domestic travel.

Expenses are approved by the claimant's line manager. Self-approval is not
permitted under any circumstances, including for department heads, whose claims
are approved by the Chief Financial Officer.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. PERFORMANCE AND PROGRESSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Performance is reviewed twice yearly, in April and October. Reviews assess delivery
against agreed objectives, technical contribution, and collaboration.

Ratings are: Exceptional, Strong, Consistent, Developing, and Below Expectations.
The Company does not operate a forced distribution.

Promotion decisions are made in the April cycle and take effect from 1 July.
Salary review occurs annually in April, effective 1 June.

Employees rated Below Expectations enter a structured support plan of 90 days with
defined objectives and fortnightly review. The plan is a genuine attempt at
improvement, not a procedural step toward dismissal.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. CODE OF CONDUCT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Employees are expected to act with integrity, treat colleagues and clients with
respect, and protect the Company's and its clients' confidential information.

The Company does not tolerate harassment, discrimination, or victimisation on any
ground. Concerns may be raised with a line manager, People Operations, or through
the confidential reporting channel described in section 11.

Conflicts of interest — including outside employment, directorships, and financial
interests in suppliers or competitors — must be declared to People Operations
within 14 days of arising.

Gifts from clients or suppliers with a value above INR 2,500 must be declared and
may not be accepted without department head approval. Cash and cash equivalents may
never be accepted, at any value.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. DISCIPLINARY AND GRIEVANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The disciplinary procedure has four stages: informal discussion, first written
warning, final written warning, and dismissal. Gross misconduct may result in
dismissal without prior warning.

Employees have the right to be accompanied by a colleague at any formal hearing and
the right to appeal any formal outcome within 10 working days.

Grievances should first be raised informally with the line manager. Where that is
inappropriate or unsuccessful, a formal grievance may be submitted in writing to
People Operations, who will appoint an investigating manager not previously
involved in the matter.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. CONFIDENTIAL REPORTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Employees who become aware of conduct that is unlawful, unsafe, or contrary to this
handbook may report it confidentially to the Company Secretary or through the
independent reporting line operated by a third-party provider.

Reports may be made anonymously. The Company will not retaliate against anyone who
raises a concern in good faith, and any attempt to do so is itself treated as gross
misconduct.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
12. LEAVING THE COMPANY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Notice periods are 30 days during probation, 60 days for employees below manager
grade, and 90 days for manager grade and above.

On the final working day, all Company property — laptop, access card, mobile
device, and any client materials — must be returned to the IT Service Desk. Access
to Company systems is revoked at 18:30 on the final working day.

Post-employment obligations regarding confidentiality and non-solicitation continue
for 12 months and are set out in the individual employment contract.

Exit interviews are offered to all leavers and are conducted by People Operations.
Participation is voluntary and the content is not shared with the line manager in
attributable form.
