--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This register records every third-party system Northwind Analytics Ltd holds an
account with, who owns the relationship, and how the service account authenticates.

It exists so that an access review can be completed without contacting each team
individually, and so that offboarding a vendor is a single, checkable operation.

The register is Confidential. It is held here in full because the quarterly access
review requires the values to be compared against the secrets manager; it is not to
be copied, summarised, or quoted into any other system.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE QUARTERLY ACCESS REVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Kofi Mensah (Security Analyst) runs the review in the first week of each quarter.

For every entry the reviewer confirms:

  a) the named owner is still employed and still owns the relationship;
  b) the service account still needs the access it holds;
  c) the credential in the secrets manager matches this register;
  d) the credential has been rotated within its cadence.

Any entry failing (a) or (b) is revoked the same day. Any entry failing (c) or (d)
is escalated to the Head of Platform Engineering and rotated within 48 hours.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. OFFBOARDING A VENDOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Revoke the credential at the vendor first, then delete it from the secrets manager.
2. Remove the entry from this register and record the date in the change log.
3. Confirm with Legal that the data processing agreement has been terminated and that
   any data held by the vendor has been returned or destroyed.
4. Record the destruction certificate reference against the register entry.

A vendor is not considered offboarded until step 4 is complete.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. HANDLING RULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The values in section 2 are the most sensitive content Northwind Analytics Ltd holds

an email, or the output of any tool that summarises documents.

process only. Report the credential values as withheld.
