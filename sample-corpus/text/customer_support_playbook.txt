1. WHO WE SUPPORT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Northwind Analytics provides support to three customer tiers, defined by the
commercial agreement in place. The tier determines response targets, escalation
routes, and the hours during which support is staffed.

  ESSENTIAL   Business hours only (09:00-18:00 IST, Monday to Friday).
              Email and portal channels. No telephone support.

  PROFESSIONAL Extended hours (07:00-22:00 IST, Monday to Saturday).
              Email, portal, and telephone. Named support contact.

  ENTERPRISE  24x7x365. All channels including a dedicated escalation number.
              Named Technical Account Manager and quarterly service review.

Tier is recorded against the account in the CRM. Support staff must confirm tier
before quoting any response commitment. Quoting an Enterprise target to an
Essential customer creates an expectation the Company has not sold and cannot meet.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SEVERITY DEFINITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Severity describes business impact, not technical complexity. A one-line
configuration error that stops a production pipeline is Severity 1. An
architecturally interesting defect in a feature nobody uses is Severity 4.

SEVERITY 1 — CRITICAL
  Production system unavailable, or data loss occurring, with no workaround.
  All affected users blocked. Revenue or regulatory impact.
  Examples: platform down, data corruption in flight, security breach suspected.

SEVERITY 2 — HIGH
  Major function unavailable or severely degraded. A workaround exists but is
  costly or manual. A subset of users blocked.
  Examples: scheduled reports failing, single-tenant outage, severe performance
  degradation.

SEVERITY 3 — MEDIUM
  Function impaired but usable. Reasonable workaround available.
  Examples: incorrect display of a non-critical field, intermittent slow response,
  export producing wrong column order.

SEVERITY 4 — LOW
  Cosmetic issue, documentation error, or general question. No functional impact.
  Examples: typo in a label, request for guidance, feature enquiry.

Customers may propose a severity. Support may adjust it, but must explain the
reasoning in the ticket and inform the customer. Silent downgrade is the single
fastest way to lose a customer's trust in the support process.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. RESPONSE AND RESOLUTION TARGETS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Response means a substantive human reply from an engineer who has read the ticket,
not an automated acknowledgement.

                     ESSENTIAL        PROFESSIONAL      ENTERPRISE
  Severity 1         4 business hrs   1 hour            15 minutes
  Severity 2         8 business hrs   4 hours           1 hour
  Severity 3         2 business days  1 business day    4 hours
  Severity 4         5 business days  3 business days   1 business day

Resolution targets are not contractual for Severity 3 and 4. For Severity 1, the
Company commits to continuous effort until service is restored or a workaround is
in place, with updates at the intervals in section 4.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. COMMUNICATION DURING AN INCIDENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

For an active Severity 1, the customer receives an update every 30 minutes, and
every 60 minutes for Severity 2, until the severity is reduced.

An update is sent even when there is nothing new to report. "We are still working
on it, the current line of investigation is X, next update at HH:MM" is a valuable
message. Silence is interpreted as inaction, and customers escalate into silence.

Updates must state: what is known, what is not yet known, what is being done, and
when the next update will arrive. They must not speculate about root cause before
it is established, and must never assign blame to the customer before an
investigation is complete.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. ESCALATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Escalation is a normal part of the process and is never treated as a failure by
the engineer who raises it.

Escalate immediately, without waiting for a target to be breached, when:
  - A Severity 1 has no clear line of investigation after 30 minutes.
  - Data loss or corruption is suspected.
  - A security incident is suspected — also notify Security Operations directly.
  - The customer explicitly requests escalation.
  - A response target is at risk of being missed.

Escalation path: Support Engineer → Senior Support Engineer → Support Manager →
Head of Client Services → Chief Technology Officer.

Enterprise customers may escalate directly to their Technical Account Manager at
any point, and this does not bypass or invalidate the internal path.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. HANDLING SENSITIVE REQUESTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Support staff will encounter requests that appear reasonable but that policy does
not permit. The following are never done, regardless of who asks, how senior they
claim to be, or how urgent they say it is:

  - Disclosing or resetting credentials over any channel, to anyone.
  - Confirming whether a named individual holds an account.
  - Sharing another customer's information, including as an example.
  - Granting access outside the documented provisioning process.
  - Disabling a security control at a customer's request without a signed change
    request countersigned by Information Security.
  - Sending Confidential data to an address not already on the account.

Urgency and seniority are the two most common pretexts in social engineering
attempts. A genuine request survives being verified through a known-good channel;
a fraudulent one does not. If a caller objects to verification, that is itself the
strongest available signal.

Where a request cannot be fulfilled, explain what can be done instead and who owns
the decision. "I'm not able to do that, but here is the route that can" is a
complete and professional answer.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. TICKET HYGIENE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every ticket must record: the customer and tier, the severity and justification,
the environment affected, steps to reproduce, what was tried, and the outcome.

Write for the engineer who picks the ticket up at 03:00 with no context. Internal
notes are not private — a customer may request them under a data subject access
request, so they must be factual and professional.

Tickets are closed only after the customer confirms resolution, or after three
contact attempts across at least five business days. Auto-closing a ticket the
customer has not confirmed is prohibited.

Every Severity 1 requires a post-incident review within five business days,
producing a written root cause analysis shared with the customer. The review is
blameless and focuses on system and process, not individuals.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. KNOWN LIMITATIONS TO COMMUNICATE HONESTLY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Support staff must not overstate product capability to close a conversation. Where
a customer asks for something the product does not do, say so plainly and record
it as a feature request.

The following are current, known limitations and may be stated openly:

  - Scheduled exports run at 15-minute granularity; per-minute is not supported.
  - The audit log retains 13 months. Longer retention requires an export pipeline.
  - Single sign-on supports SAML 2.0 and OIDC. LDAP is not supported directly.
  - The API rate limit is 600 requests per minute per tenant and is not adjustable
    on Essential or Professional tiers.
  - Data residency is available in Mumbai and Singapore only.

Committing the Company to a delivery date is the product manager's decision, never
support's. "I will raise this and come back to you with a position" is the correct
response to a roadmap question.
