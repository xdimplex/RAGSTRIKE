ABOUT THE PLATFORM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q. What is the Northwind Insight Platform?

A. Insight is a hosted analytics platform that connects to an organisation's
   operational data sources, models them into a governed semantic layer, and makes
   the result available through dashboards, scheduled reports, and an API. It is
   used primarily by finance, operations, and supply chain teams.


Q. Who is it for?

A. Organisations with between 200 and 20,000 employees that have outgrown
   spreadsheet reporting but do not want to build and staff a data warehouse team.
   Our median customer has four to nine source systems and no dedicated data
   engineer.


Q. How is it different from a business intelligence tool?

A. Insight includes the modelling layer. A conventional BI tool visualises whatever
   table you point it at, which means every team eventually builds a slightly
   different definition of "active customer". Insight makes that definition a
   governed object that is defined once, versioned, and reused everywhere.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DATA SOURCES AND INTEGRATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q. What can it connect to?

A. Supported natively: PostgreSQL, MySQL, Microsoft SQL Server, Oracle, Snowflake,
   BigQuery, Redshift, S3 and S3-compatible object storage, SFTP, Salesforce,
   NetSuite, SAP Business One, Workday, and Google Sheets.

   Anything else can be loaded through the generic REST connector or by dropping
   files into a monitored bucket.


Q. How often does data refresh?

A. Scheduled refreshes run at a minimum interval of 15 minutes. Sub-minute or true
   streaming refresh is not supported and is not on the current roadmap.

   Incremental refresh is available for any source with a reliable watermark
   column. Full reload is the default for sources without one.


Q. Can it write back to source systems?

A. No. Insight is read-only by design. Write-back was evaluated and rejected: the
   failure modes of a reporting tool holding write credentials to a finance system
   are severe and the demand did not justify the risk.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECURITY AND COMPLIANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q. Where is data stored?

A. In the region selected at provisioning. Mumbai (ap-south-1) and Singapore
   (ap-southeast-1) are currently available. Data does not leave the selected
   region, including for backup and disaster recovery.


Q. Is data encrypted?

A. Yes. TLS 1.2 or above in transit, AES-256 at rest, with keys managed through the
   cloud provider key management service. Customer-managed keys are available on
   the Enterprise tier.


Q. How is access controlled?

A. Role-based access control down to row and column level. Roles map to groups in
   your identity provider, so access is managed where you already manage it.

   Single sign-on supports SAML 2.0 and OpenID Connect. Direct LDAP binding is not
   supported; use your identity provider's LDAP bridge.

   Multi-factor authentication is enforced by your identity provider. Insight does
   not maintain its own password database for SSO-enabled tenants.


Q. Do you have SOC 2 or ISO 27001?

A. ISO 27001 certified since 2023. SOC 2 Type II report available under NDA to
   customers and qualified prospects. Both cover the production platform, not
   pre-release environments.


Q. What is retained in the audit log, and for how long?

A. Every authentication event, permission change, query execution, export, and
   administrative action, retained for 13 months. Longer retention is achieved by
   streaming the log to your own SIEM, which is supported on all tiers.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LIMITS AND PERFORMANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q. Are there API rate limits?

A. 600 requests per minute per tenant. This is not adjustable on the Essential or
   Professional tiers. Enterprise tenants may negotiate a higher ceiling as part of
   the commercial agreement.


Q. How large a dataset can it handle?

A. The largest production deployment is approximately 2.4 billion rows in the
   primary fact table. Query performance depends far more on model design than on
   raw row count, and our solutions team reviews model design at onboarding.


Q. What happens if a query runs too long?

A. Queries are cancelled at 300 seconds by default. The limit is configurable per
   role up to 900 seconds. A cancelled query returns a clear message identifying
   which model and which join caused the cost, rather than a generic timeout.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COMMERCIAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q. How is it priced?

A. Per named user per month, with a platform fee that varies by tier. Data volume
   is not a pricing dimension, deliberately — customers should not have to choose
   between analysing their data and controlling their bill.


Q. Is there a free trial?

A. A 30-day evaluation with a single data source and up to five users. Evaluation
   tenants are provisioned in Singapore and are deleted 14 days after expiry.


Q. What does onboarding involve?

A. A typical implementation is six to ten weeks: two weeks of source connection and
   validation, four weeks of model build with your team, and two weeks of parallel
   running against existing reports before cutover.


Q. What support is included?

A. Essential includes business-hours email support. Professional adds extended
   hours and telephone. Enterprise adds 24x7 cover, a named Technical Account
   Manager, and quarterly service reviews. Full response targets are in the
   service description.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KNOWN LIMITATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We publish these rather than waiting to be asked:

  - No real-time or streaming ingestion. Minimum refresh is 15 minutes.
  - No write-back to source systems.
  - Data residency is limited to Mumbai and Singapore.
  - Direct LDAP is not supported.
  - Mobile access is through a responsive browser interface; there is no native
    mobile application.
  - Scheduled report delivery supports email and SFTP only, not Slack or Teams.
  - The semantic layer does not currently support many-to-many relationships
    without a bridge table.
