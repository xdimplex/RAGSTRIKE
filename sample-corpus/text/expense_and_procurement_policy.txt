NORTHWIND ANALYTICS LTD
EXPENSE AND PROCUREMENT POLICY
Document reference: NW-FIN-003  ·  Revision 4  ·  Owner: Finance
Classification: Internal

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SCOPE AND PRINCIPLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This policy governs how Northwind Analytics Ltd commits and reimburses money. It
applies to every employee and contractor who incurs a cost on the Company's behalf,
at any value.

Three principles underpin every rule that follows:

  NECESSARY   The cost supports a genuine business purpose that would not have
              been achieved without it.

  REASONABLE  A prudent person spending their own money would have made the same
              choice in the same circumstances.

  EVIDENCED   The cost is supported by a valid document showing supplier, date,
              amount, tax, and what was purchased.

Where a rule below does not cover a situation, apply the three principles and
record the reasoning in the claim. Finance would rather review a well-explained
judgement than a technically compliant one that makes no sense.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. AUTHORITY LIMITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Commitment authority is the value a role may commit the Company to without a
higher approval. Limits apply per transaction and may not be split to stay under a
threshold. Splitting a purchase to avoid an approval level is treated as a
disciplinary matter, not an administrative one.

  Team Lead                        up to INR      50,000
  Manager                          up to INR     200,000
  Department Head                  up to INR   1,000,000
  Chief Financial Officer          up to INR   5,000,000
  Executive Committee              up to INR  20,000,000
  Board                            above INR  20,000,000

Multi-year agreements are assessed on total contract value, not annual value. A
three-year subscription at INR 400,000 per year is a INR 1,200,000 commitment and
requires CFO approval.

Self-approval is prohibited at every level without exception. A department head's
own expenses are approved by the CFO; the CFO's are approved by the Chief Executive.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. TRAVEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Travel is booked through the Company travel desk wherever possible. Direct booking
is permitted where the travel desk cannot meet a requirement, and the reason is
recorded in the claim.

Air travel
  Economy class for scheduled flight time under six hours.
  Premium economy permitted above six hours with department head approval.
  Business class requires Executive Committee approval and is never approved for
  domestic sectors.
  Tickets are booked at least 14 days in advance where the trip is known; late
  booking requires a note explaining why.

Rail
  Any class up to and including 2AC. 1AC requires manager approval.

Accommodation
  Domestic, per night                        INR   6,500
  Metro (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru)       INR   9,000
  International, per night                   USD     180

  Where a conference venue rate exceeds these limits, the conference rate is
  acceptable with the booking confirmation attached.

Ground transport
  Metered taxi, ride-hailing, or public transport. Reimbursed at actual cost.
  Private car use is reimbursed at INR 12 per kilometre, inclusive of fuel and
  wear. A route record is required for claims above 100 km.

Subsistence
  Meals while travelling, per day            INR   2,000
  International, per day                     USD      65

  This is a ceiling on actual spend, not a per-diem allowance. Unspent amounts are
  not payable.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. ENTERTAINMENT AND HOSPITALITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Client entertainment is permitted up to INR 3,500 per head with prior manager
approval. The claim must name every attendee and their organisation. A claim
recording "client dinner, 6 people" without names will be rejected.

Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of a client meal, and only where it is a
minority of the bill. Alcohol on personal travel is never reimbursable.

Team events are budgeted annually by department at INR 3,000 per head per quarter
and do not require individual approval within that budget.

Gifts to clients or public officials of any value require prior written approval
from the Company Secretary. Cash and cash equivalents may never be given, at any
value, to anyone, under any circumstances. This is an anti-bribery control and is
not subject to local custom or commercial pressure.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. SOFTWARE, SUBSCRIPTIONS, AND CLOUD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Any software or service that will process Company or client data requires
Information Security review before purchase, regardless of cost — including free
tiers, trials, and personal accounts used for work.

This applies without exception to AI and large language model services. Entering
Confidential information into a public assistant is a reportable data breach, and
a free trial is not exempt from review because it did not cost anything.

Recurring subscriptions are owned by a named individual who is responsible for
annual review. Unowned subscriptions are cancelled at renewal.

Cloud infrastructure spend is governed by team budgets with alerts at 70%, 90%,
and 100% of the monthly allocation. Sustained overspend requires a written
explanation to the CFO, not a budget increase by default.

Personal purchase of software for reimbursement is discouraged and requires
department head approval, because it bypasses licence management and security
review.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. SUBMITTING A CLAIM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Claims are submitted through the finance portal within 60 days of the expense.
Claims beyond 60 days require department head approval and beyond 120 days are
not payable.

Every claim line requires a legible receipt or tax invoice. A card statement is
not a receipt: it shows an amount, not what was purchased. Where a receipt is
genuinely unobtainable, a signed missing-receipt declaration may be used up to
INR 2,000, no more than three times per employee per year.

Foreign currency is converted at the card statement rate where the Company card
was used, or at the RBI reference rate on the transaction date otherwise.

Approved claims are paid in the next payroll run, which is on the 25th of each
month for claims approved by the 18th.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. WHAT IS NOT REIMBURSABLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The following are not reimbursable under any circumstances:

  - Traffic fines, parking penalties, and speeding charges.
  - Personal travel extensions, and companion travel costs.
  - Airport lounge access, unless included in the fare.
  - In-flight entertainment, minibar, and hotel movies.
  - Personal grooming, laundry for trips under four nights, and clothing.
  - Loss of personal property, including devices and luggage.
  - Political donations of any kind.
  - Charitable donations made personally, whatever the cause.
  - Costs already covered by a client under an engagement agreement.

Where a claim mixes reimbursable and non-reimbursable lines, the reimbursable
portion is paid and the remainder rejected with an explanation. The whole claim is
not refused for one bad line.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. AUDIT AND ASSURANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Finance samples 10% of claims each month for detailed review, weighted toward
higher values and toward claimants with prior rejections.

The full expense population is reviewed annually by internal audit and is in scope
for the external statutory audit.

Deliberate submission of a false claim is gross misconduct and will result in
dismissal and, where the value warrants it, referral to the police. This has
happened and the Company will not treat it as a private matter.

Honest error carries no penalty. An employee who identifies their own mistake and
reports it to Finance will have it corrected without consequence.
